Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this web app is to keep track of your tasks for the day and categorize them into sections, then you can finish those tasks.

Audience

People who need help with their productivity tasks and want a fun solution to their to-do lists.

Dynamic elements

My example of dynamics elements is the randomly selected task, the leveling up task categories, and the reactive buttons changing the screen.
