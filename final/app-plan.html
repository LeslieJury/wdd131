Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this web app is to keep track of your tasks for the day and categorize them into sections, then you can level up those sections.

Audience

People who need help with their productivity tasks and want a fun solution to their to-do lists.

Dynamic elements

The randomly selected task, the leveling up task categories.
